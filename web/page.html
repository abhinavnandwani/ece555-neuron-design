Why carry-save, and how it differs from the spec diagram

  • Every product and the bias is −1, 0 or +1: a nonzero bit and a negative bit, so each value is nonzero − 2·negative.
  • The sum is (number of nonzero terms) − 2·(number of negative terms). One full adder counts each, and the two run in parallel with no carry between them.
  • Two gates turn the counts into the ReLU output directly: out[1] = nz.carry · !neg.sum · !neg.carry (NOR3) and out[0] = nz.sum · !neg.carry · (nz.carry + !neg.sum) (AOI211). out[2] is always 0.
  • This only works because values are limited to −1, 0 and +1. Slide 14 calls its block list an example, but MS1 asks for adder and mux schematics, so the course schematic requirements still need to be reconciled with this architecture.

How far to trust the model

Fitted to 7,764 Spectre transitions in 20 circuits, with the PMOS/NMOS ratio fixed at the measured drain-current ratio of 1.20 (49.35 µA / 41.19 µA). Every circuit's worst-case delay is within 8.5% of Spectre; the model reads about 5% low on average, and it orders circuit pairs the same way Spectre does. Before refitting, it predicted the 14 carry-save sweep circuits it had never seen to within 15%.

Use it to narrow choices and explain trends. Differences under about 10% need Spectre, and it over-credits upsizing only some gates.

Remaining validation

  1. Extract interconnect and device parasitics and rerun the complete fixed-weight test history, especially mirror 2:3 versus 3:3. LVS connectivity extraction is not an RC timing result.
  2. Compact and route alternative layouts, then remeasure their boundaries and repeat DRC/LVS. Single-row placement is not area-optimal.
  3. Measure more beta-grid points if their delay and likely area justify them. The other 58 points cannot yet be ranked by measured T × A.
  4. Repeat load, slew and PVT sensitivity at the finalist sizings, and establish the course measurement conditions. The 2:2 sensitivity controls do not establish 2:3 robustness.
  5. Complete student schematics, submodule evidence, extracted-netlist timing and the course report. These experiments do not establish milestone submission or completion.
